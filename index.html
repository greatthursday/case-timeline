How to use Case Timeline

On a Mac trackpad, right-click is a two-finger click or Control-click.

On the chart

  • Click an event box to open it in the Events tab on the right.
  • Drag an event box to move its label. The time stays where it is, and nearby lines make room.
  • Right-click an event box to highlight it, merge it with the box to its left or right, split a shared box, reset its position, tidy its line, or delete it.
  • Right-click empty space on a line to add an event at that time, or to tidy that line.
  • Click the title, subtitle, case number, prepared by, or footer to edit it in place. The change shows on every page. Press Enter to keep it or Esc to cancel.
  • Click a black section heading to rename that section.
  • Click the legend to open the Legend tab. Click a line's date label to open the Sections tab.
  • Click a row in the Contents table to jump to that section. In a saved PDF these are links, and the title on later pages links back to the contents.

Tidying lines

  • Shrink pulls moved boxes back toward their line, keeping each one above or below and at its left-right spot.
  • Optimize rearranges every box on a line so the line is as short as possible.
  • Reset puts boxes back where the app would place them.
  • Use them on one line from the right-click menu, or on every line from the Page tab under Spacing.

Highlighting

  • Right-click an event and choose Highlight, or pick a color under Highlight in the Events tab. Yellow is the default. Other picks any color.
  • Each color used gets its own legend entry. Rename it in the Legend tab, for example "Seen on camera".
  • In Excel, the Highlight column takes Yellow, Green, Blue, Pink, Orange, or Purple.

The tabs on the right

  • Events: edit the selected event, or click any event in the list.
  • Sections: set each section's dates and how much time one line covers. Drag sections to reorder them, and untick one to leave it out.
  • Legend: rename icons and chart marks, and add icons from the library.
  • Page: title, footer, paper size, and spacing.
  • Map: export locations for a mapping tool.

Saving and printing

  • Changes save to the case's Excel file on their own. The status at the top says when. Close the file in Excel while you work here.
  • Print / Save PDF, then choose Save as PDF, makes the file for the DA.
